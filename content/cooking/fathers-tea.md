## The story

The first thing I learned to cook was tea. My father taught me, so the recipe carries more than measurements: it carries the beginning of learning through family.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- Tea leaves, according to strength
- Milk, according to taste
- Sugar, if wanted

## Method

1. Bring the water to a gentle boil.
2. Add the tea leaves and let the colour deepen.
3. Add milk and sugar, then lower the heat.
4. Strain into a favourite cup and serve warm.

## Serving note

Best shared slowly—with family, conversation, and no need to rush.
